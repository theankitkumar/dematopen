# DematOpen.com – Business Description & Compliance Framework

## 1. Nature of Business

This platform operates as an independent facilitation and informational service that assists users in initiating the process of opening demat and trading accounts with Upstox (RKSV Securities India Private Limited), a SEBI-regulated stockbroker.

The platform functions in the capacity of an Authorized Person associated with the aforesaid broker and enables users to understand the account opening process and proceed through official channels.

---

## 2. Scope of Services

The services provided by this platform are strictly limited to:

* Providing general information about demat and trading accounts
* Assisting users in initiating the account opening process
* Redirecting or connecting users to the official onboarding system of Upstox
* Offering basic guidance related to documentation and onboarding steps

This platform does NOT directly open accounts. All accounts are opened and maintained by Upstox.

---

## 3. Non-Advisory Declaration

This platform does not provide:

* Investment advice
* Stock recommendations or tips
* Portfolio management services
* Financial planning or advisory services

All decisions made by users are at their own discretion and risk.

---

## 4. Relationship with Upstox

This platform operates as an Authorized Person associated with Upstox (RKSV Securities India Private Limited).

However:

* This platform is NOT owned, operated, or controlled by Upstox
* It acts as an independent facilitator
* Any services availed by users are subject to Upstox’s terms, policies, and verification processes

---

## 5. Revenue Model

This platform may earn a commission or referral incentive from Upstox for successful account openings completed through its referral system.

No additional fees are charged directly to users by this platform for account opening assistance.

---

## 6. User Interaction & Support

* For account opening or operational support, users are directed to contact Upstox directly via their official support channels
* This platform does not handle sensitive account-level operations such as fund transfers, profile changes, or trading activities
* Users are encouraged to use official Upstox channels for all transactional or account-related queries

---

## 7. Compliance with Upstox Partner Policies

This platform adheres to all applicable policies defined for Authorized Partners, including but not limited to:

* Not requesting or modifying referral codes on behalf of users
* Not bidding on restricted brand keywords in paid marketing campaigns
* Ensuring users directly interact with official Upstox systems for account-related actions
* Not engaging in misleading marketing practices

---

## 8. Regulatory Compliance

This platform is designed to align with:

* SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) regulations
* NSE and BSE guidelines
* Applicable financial advertising standards

All content is structured to ensure transparency, non-misleading communication, and user awareness.

---

## 9. Risk Disclosure

Investments in securities markets are subject to market risks. Users are advised to carefully read all relevant documents and disclosures provided by the broker before making any investment decisions.

Past performance is not indicative of future results.

---

## 10. Limitation of Liability

This platform shall not be held liable for:

* Any financial losses incurred by users
* Decisions made based on informational content
* Delays or issues arising from third-party systems (including Upstox)

Users acknowledge that all financial activities are conducted directly with the broker.

---

## 11. Data Handling & Privacy

This platform may collect basic user information solely for the purpose of facilitating onboarding.

Sensitive financial data, KYC verification, and account credentials are handled exclusively by Upstox through their official systems.

---

## 12. Ethical Marketing Commitment

This platform ensures that:

* No misleading claims or guaranteed returns are promoted
* No impersonation of the broker occurs
* Clear distinction is maintained between the platform and Upstox
* All advertisements accurately reflect the nature of services

---

## 13. Platform Positioning Summary

This platform is:

* An independent facilitation service
* An Authorized Person-associated onboarding channel
* A non-advisory, non-transactional interface

This platform is NOT:

* A stockbroker
* A financial advisor
* A trading platform
* The official website of Upstox

---

## 14. Continuous Compliance

Policies, disclaimers, and content on this platform may be updated periodically to ensure continued compliance with regulatory and partner guidelines.

Users are encouraged to review policies regularly.
