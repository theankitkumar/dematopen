What is a Demat Account?

Your digital locker for stocks — safe, secure, and mandatory for trading in India.

Simple Definition

A Demat Account is a digital locker where all your shares, bonds, mutual funds, and investments are stored electronically. No physical certificates — everything is digital and instantly accessible.

Why You Need It

In India, it's mandatory to have a Demat account to buy or sell shares. Just like you need a bank account for money — you need a Demat account for stocks.
