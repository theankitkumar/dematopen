Step 01 — Basics Samjhe

Demat account kya hota hai?

Simple bole toh — yeh ek digital locker hai jisme aapke shares safe rakhe jaate hain. Bilkul bank account jaisa — bas paise ki jagah shares store hote hain.

Bank Account

Paise rakhne ke liye hota hai. Jab chaho daalo, jab chaho nikalo.

  • Cash / digital paisa rehta hai
  • Withdraw kabhi bhi kar sakte ho

Demat Account

Bilkul vaisa hi — lekin isme shares rakhe jaate hain, paise nahi.

  • Digital form me shares rehte hain
  • NSDL / CDSL me secure

Bas itna hi. Aur kuch nahi.

Confusion Door Kare

Demat vs Trading Account?

Yeh do alag cheezein hain — aur aapko dono chahiye. Tension nahi, dono saath me free milte hain.

Trading Account

Yeh action wala account hai. Shares buy aur sell karne ke liye.

  • Order place karta hai
  • Bank account se linked
  • Buy / sell ka kaam

Demat Account

Yeh storage wala account hai. Shares rakhne ke liye.

  • Shares store karta hai
  • NSDL / CDSL me safe
  • Ownership ka proof

Ek line me samjho

Trading account dukaan ka counter hai — Demat account dukaan ki almari. Aajkal har broker dono saath me free deta hai.

Common Doubts

Beginner ke sabse common sawaal

Kya yeh safe hai? Paisa doob toh nahi jayega?
Bilkul safe hai. Demat account SEBI ke under regulated hai, aur aapke shares NSDL/CDSL (government-backed depositories) me rehte hain. Stock prices upar-neeche hote hain, lekin long-term me achhi companies me invest karne walo ke liye history achhi rahi hai. Tension kam karne ke liye — chhote amount se start kare.
Kitna paisa chahiye start karne ke liye?
Sirf ₹100 se. Bahut shares ka price isse kam hota hai. Ya ₹500/month ki SIP mutual funds me — same demat app se. Jitni jaldi shuru, compounding ka faayda utna zyada.
Free hai ya charge lagta hai?
Top brokers (jaise Upstox) account opening ke liye ₹0 charge karte hain. Ek chhota annual maintenance fee (AMC) doosre saal se lagta hai, aur trades pe brokerage — sab pehle se clearly likha hota hai. Koi surprise nahi.
Hidden charges toh nahi hain?
Reputable SEBI-registered brokers har fee transparently dikhate hain — brokerage, AMC, GST, STT (govt tax), exchange charges. Pricing page ek baar zaroor padhe. Established platforms me "surprise" deduction nahi hote.
Kya beginner really start kar sakta hai?
Bilkul. Modern apps beginners ke liye hi banaye gaye hain — clean design, simple words, in-app learning. Finance degree nahi chahiye. Pehle 1 share kharide kisi company ka jo aap already use karte ho (Reliance, ITC, HDFC Bank) — aur saath-saath seekhe.
Agar loss ho gaya toh?
Short-term me upar-neeche normal hai — pros ke saath bhi hota hai. Trick: long-term ke liye invest kare (5+ saal), paisa alag-alag companies me spread kare, panic me sell na kare. Chhote amount se shuru kare jiska risk aap emotionally handle kar sako.
