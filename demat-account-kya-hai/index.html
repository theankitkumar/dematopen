Common Doubts

Beginner ke sabse common sawaal

Kya yeh safe hai?
Bilkul safe hai — Demat account SEBI ke under regulated hai, aur aapke shares NSDL/CDSL (government-backed depositories) me rehte hain. Stock price upar-neeche hote rehte hain, lekin long-term me achhi companies me invest karne walon ke liye history achhi rahi hai. Tension kam karne ke liye chhote amount se start kare.
Kitna paisa chahiye start karne ke liye?
Sirf ₹100 se shuru kare. Bahut shares ka price isse kam hota hai. Ya phir ₹500/month ki SIP mutual funds me — same demat app se. Jitni jaldi shuru karenge, compounding ka faayda utna zyada milega.
Free hai ya nahi?
Haan — top brokers (jaise Upstox) account opening ke liye ₹0 charge karte hain. Ek chhota annual maintenance fee (AMC) doosre saal se hota hai, aur trades pe brokerage — sab pehle se clearly mention hota hai. Koi surprise nahi.
Hidden charges hai kya?
Reputable SEBI-registered brokers har fee transparently dikhate hain — brokerage, AMC, GST, STT (govt tax), exchange charges. Pricing page ek baar zaroor padhe. Established platforms (Upstox, Zerodha) me "surprise" deduction nahi hote.
Beginner kya start kar sakta hai?
Bilkul. Modern apps beginners ke liye banaye gaye hain — clean design, simple words, in-app learning. Finance ki degree nahi chahiye. Pehle 1 share kharide kisi company ka jo aap already use karte ho (Reliance, ITC, HDFC Bank) — aur saath saath seekhe.
Agar paisa loss ho gaya toh?
Short-term me upar-neeche normal hai — pros ke saath bhi hota hai. Trick yeh hai: long-term ke liye invest kare (5+ saal), paisa alag-alag companies me spread kare, aur panic me sell na kare. Chhote amount se shuru kare jiska upar-neeche hona aap emotionally handle kar sake.
