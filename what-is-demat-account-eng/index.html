QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Common questions beginners ask

Is investing in stocks actually safe?
The platform itself is very safe — Demat accounts are SEBI-regulated, and your shares sit with NSDL/CDSL (government-backed depositories). Stock prices can rise and fall, but for long-term investors in good companies, the market has historically grown. Start small while you learn.
How much money do I need to start?
As little as ₹100. Many shares cost less than that. Or start a SIP in mutual funds for ₹500/month from the same Demat app. The earlier you start — even with small amounts — the more compounding works for you.
Is opening a Demat account really free?
Yes — top brokers like Upstox charge ₹0 for opening. There's a small annual maintenance fee (AMC) typically charged from the second year, plus brokerage on trades — all clearly listed upfront. No surprises.
Are there any hidden charges?
Reputable SEBI-registered brokers list every fee transparently — brokerage, AMC, GST, STT (govt tax), exchange charges. Always read the pricing page once. With established platforms, no "surprise" deductions appear.
Can a complete beginner really start?
Absolutely. Modern apps are built specifically for beginners — clean design, simple words, in-app learning. You don't need a finance degree. Buy 1 share of a company you already use (Reliance, ITC, HDFC Bank) and learn as you go.
What if I lose money?
Short-term ups and downs are normal — even pros face them. The trick: invest for the long term (5+ years), spread money across different companies, and don't panic-sell. Start with small amounts you're emotionally okay seeing fluctuate.
