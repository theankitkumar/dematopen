QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Got questions? We've got answers

What is a Demat account and why do I need it?
A Demat account (Dematerialized account) is a digital wallet for your shares and securities. Just like you store money in a bank account, a Demat account stores your stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and other investments electronically. It's mandatory in India to have a Demat account to buy or sell shares — without it, you cannot trade or invest in stocks, mutual funds, or IPOs.
Is investing in stocks actually safe?
The platform is very safe — your shares are stored with NSDL/CDSL (government-backed depositories) and the broker is SEBI-regulated. Stock prices can rise and fall in the short term, but historically, the Indian stock market has given positive returns over the long term (5+ years). Start small, invest regularly through SIPs, and focus on long-term wealth creation.
How much money do I need to start investing?
As little as ₹100. There's no minimum balance requirement for your Demat account. Many shares cost less than ₹100. For mutual funds, SIP can start from just ₹100/month — perfect for beginners. The earlier you start, the more compounding works for you.
What documents do I need to open a Demat account?
You need: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card (mobile-linked), Bank account details (cancelled cheque or statement), and a signature on white paper. Everything is uploaded digitally. The entire process takes 15 minutes — no physical paperwork required.
Are there any hidden charges?
No hidden charges, ever. Our pricing: ₹0 account opening, ₹0 AMC for first year, ₹0 equity delivery brokerage, ₹0 mutual funds & IPO commission, max ₹20/order on intraday & F&O. Government taxes (STT, GST, exchange charges) apply as per regulation — these are listed transparently before every trade.
What's the difference between Demat and Trading account?
Demat account stores your shares (like a wardrobe). Trading account is used to buy/sell shares (like the checkout counter). You need both to invest — and we provide both, completely FREE, in the same signup.
What are mutual funds & SIPs? Good for beginners?
Mutual funds are professionally managed schemes where money from many investors is pooled and invested in stocks/bonds. SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) lets you invest a fixed amount monthly (from ₹100). Both are perfect for beginners — automatic diversification, professional management, and compounding works in your favor. We charge ₹0 commission on mutual funds.
What is an IPO? Can beginners apply?
IPO (Initial Public Offering) is when a company sells its shares to the public for the first time. Yes, beginners can apply through our app — one-tap UPI application, ₹0 commission. If allotted, shares get credited to your Demat account on listing day.
When is the stock market open?
Pre-market: 9:00 AM – 9:15 AM. Regular trading: 9:15 AM – 3:30 PM (Mon–Fri). Post-market: 3:40 PM – 4:00 PM. Closed on weekends and public holidays. You can place orders 24/7 — they execute during market hours.
How do I add money to my account?
Multiple options: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm — instant), NetBanking (instant), NEFT/RTGS (2–4 hours), Cheque deposit (1–2 working days). All transfers are free — no charges.
Do I pay taxes on stock market gains?
Yes. Short-term gains (sold within 1 year) taxed at 15%. Long-term gains (sold after 1 year) taxed at 10% on gains above ₹1 lakh/year. Dividend income is added to your slab. Tip: hold stocks 1+ year for lower long-term tax rate. Our app provides ready capital gains statements for your tax filing.
Can I transfer shares from another broker?
Yes, easily. Use a DIS (Delivery Instruction Slip) with your current broker, provide our Demat details, and shares transfer in 3–5 working days. No tax implications, no market risk during transfer.
What if I want to close my account later?
Closure is simple and free. Sell or transfer your holdings, withdraw funds, submit a closure request via app/support. Account closes in 7–10 working days. No exit fees, no hassle.
